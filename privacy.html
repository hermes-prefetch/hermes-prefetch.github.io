Privacy policy

Hermes Prefetch accesses Google user data only for its single owner, using read-only Gmail and Google Calendar scopes.

Use: message and event text is read to produce a private daily summary for the owner. Summaries are generated with a third-party AI model provider under a zero-data-retention setting.

Storage: data is kept on the owner's own devices.

Sharing: data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than the model provider described above.

The app cannot send, modify or delete email or calendar data.
